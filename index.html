01 / CAPABILITIES

Built to inspect.
Designed to inform.

NOCAM brings useful network camera auditing capabilities into one Android application, with a focus on clear findings and authorized security assessment.

⌘

Local Network Discovery

Identify devices on your local network and understand which hosts are responding to permitted discovery checks.

↗

TCP Service Checks

Inspect selected TCP ports to identify exposed services and build a clearer picture of your network.

◉

RTSP Inspection

Check RTSP service responses and identify protocol behaviour without attempting unauthorized access.

◎

ONVIF Discovery

Discover compatible ONVIF devices on supported local networks and inspect their discovery responses.

⌁

TLS Inspection

Review supported TLS connection details and certificate information for security assessment.

▤

Security Reports

Organize assessment findings into readable evidence-based reports for further investigation.

02 / WORKFLOW

Straight to the
point.

A practical workflow that keeps network inspection understandable, organized and focused.

STEP / 01

Discover

Start with a network you own or are authorized to assess. Identify responding devices and relevant network details.

STEP / 02

Inspect

Run compatible service and protocol checks to understand what is exposed and how services respond.

STEP / 03

Review

Examine the collected evidence, understand the findings and use the results to improve network security.
